Privacy Policy

Czarniak Residence · Garage Door Alerts SMS Program

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Czarniak Residence handles information in connection with its private garage-door SMS alert program.

Information we collect

We collect only the mobile phone numbers of household members who enroll to receive alerts. We do not collect payment information, location data, or any other personal information through this program.

How we use it

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver automated garage-door status alerts and closed confirmations to the residents who requested them. We do not use them for marketing.

No sharing of mobile information.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We do not sell, rent, or trade phone numbers or message content to anyone.

Data retention

Phone numbers are stored only within our private home-automation system for as long as a recipient remains enrolled. Replying STOP removes the number from active messaging.

Your choices

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP or contact us for assistance.
