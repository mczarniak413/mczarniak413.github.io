Privacy Policy

Program: CZ HA Triggers · Last updated: June 28, 2026

CZ HA Triggers ("the Program") is a personal, non-commercial home automation notification service operated by an individual for the sole purpose of sending SMS alerts to members of their own household.

What information we collect

The Program collects only the mobile phone numbers of household members who have agreed to receive automated alerts (for example, notifications that a garage door has been left open). No other personal information is collected through this Program.

How we use your information

Mobile phone numbers are used exclusively to deliver automated SMS notifications related to home automation events. Message frequency varies based on triggered events and is generally infrequent (typically fewer than 10 messages per month).

Information sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent mobile phone numbers or any other personal information collected through this Program to any third party for marketing or any other purposes. Phone numbers are used solely to deliver the notifications described above via our SMS provider (Twilio).

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies depending on home automation activity.

Opting out

You may opt out of receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You may also contact the Program operator directly to be removed.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to request removal from the Program, contact the Program operator directly.

This page is provided to satisfy carrier and A2P 10DLC registration requirements for a personal home automation SMS notification system.
