Garage Door Alerts — SMS Program

Czarniak Residence · Private home-automation notifications

This page describes the SMS text-message alert program operated by the Czarniak Residence for the members of its household. It exists so recipients understand exactly what messages they will receive and how they consent to receive them.

What this program does

Our home-automation system automatically sends a text message to household members when a residential garage door has been left open for an extended period. The alert includes a secure link to close the door remotely, and a follow-up text confirms once the door is closed.

  • Message types: automated garage-door-open alerts and closed confirmations only.
  • Message frequency: varies — messages are sent only when a door is left open, typically a few times per week or fewer.
  • No marketing. This program sends no promotional or marketing content of any kind.

Who receives messages

Recipients are exclusively the residents of the property (the homeowner and immediate family members) who own and operate this system. There is no public sign-up, no purchased lists, and no third-party recipients.

How you opt in

Each recipient enrolls their own mobile number directly by adding it to the household system and confirming the consent statement below. Consent is given actively by the person who owns the phone.

Opting out & help

  • Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe and stop all further texts.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at the email below.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.
