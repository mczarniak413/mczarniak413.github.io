Private home-automation & security notification service

The Czarniak Residence operates a private home-automation system for its household in Boulder City, Nevada. As part of that system, we send automated SMS text alerts to household members about the status of home security devices — primarily residential garage doors.

Our SMS alert program

Our system automatically texts household members when a garage door is left open for an extended period. The alert includes a secure link to close the door remotely, and a follow-up text confirms once the door is closed.

  • Message types: automated garage-door-open security alerts and closed confirmations only.
  • Frequency: varies — sent only when a door is left open, typically a few times per week or fewer.
  • No marketing: this program sends no promotional or marketing messages of any kind.
  • Recipients: household residents only — no public sign-up, no purchased lists, no third parties.
