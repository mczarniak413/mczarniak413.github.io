Czarniak Residence · Garage Door Alerts SMS Program

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Program description

The Czarniak Residence garage-door SMS program sends automated text alerts to enrolled household members when a residential garage door is left open, including a secure link to close it, plus a confirmation once the door is closed. This is a private, non-commercial notification service.

Message frequency & cost

  • Message frequency varies; messages are sent only when a garage door is left open.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-in and opt-out

  • Recipients enroll by adding their own mobile number to the system and agreeing to the consent statement on the opt-in page.
  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe from all messages.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email the contact below.
